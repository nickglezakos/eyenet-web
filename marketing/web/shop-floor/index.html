Application Deep Dive

Capabilities and target users for this story

Shop managers need how customers move through the premises — not only that someone entered. EyeNet turns existing cameras into counts, paths, heatmaps, dwell, and occupancy with behavioural alerts. Video stays in-store.

Key Features:

  • People counting — Face / Body / Both across entrances and floor cameras.
  • Paths & heatmaps — How customers move through aisles and zones.
  • Occupancy & peaks — Busy hours by area, not guesswork.
  • Behavioural alerts — Occupancy / dwell / path / co-presence → mobile, email, webhook.
  • On-prem privacy — Video stays local; managers get digests and alerts.

Target Users:

Shop managers, store operations, and multi-site retail networks.

Available Modes:

Gate, Spot, Area
