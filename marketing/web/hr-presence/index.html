Application Deep Dive

Capabilities and target users for this wedge

One Presence tool that stretches from an intelligent doorbell to maritime and industrial posts. EyeNet shows who is in, where, and when — automatically — and keeps CRM grants aligned.

Key Features:

  • Intelligent doorbell — Who is at the door at home; presence without cloud video.
  • Entrance / exit stations — Presence stations at doors for check-in / check-out.
  • Vprofile cameras — Offices, warehouses, industrial floors, and watch posts.
  • Presence apps — Staff mobile phones and desk PCs.
  • HubSpot CRM — Sync contacts and post grants in real time.

Target Users:

Homes, corporate offices, warehouses, industrial facilities, maritime operators, and security firms.

Available Modes:

Area, Gate, Spot
