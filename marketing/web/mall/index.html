Application Deep Dive

Capabilities and target users for this wedge

Static screens burn budget because nobody can prove who saw what. EyeNet reads the room and drives playlists from live audience signals — views, dwell, and engagement as a measurable marketing studio.

Key Features:

  • Read the room — Age, gender, group size drive playlist switches in milliseconds.
  • Zero surveys — Know who sees each display: views, dwell, engagement.
  • A/B in the wild — Swap content on live audience data; measure what converts.
  • Prove ROI — Impression reports, engagement rates, footfall-to-sales funnels.

Target Users:

Retail shops, shopping malls, airports, advertising agencies, event venues, and marketing firms.

Available Modes:

Spot, Gate, Area
