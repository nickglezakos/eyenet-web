Application Deep Dive

Capabilities and target users for this story

Families need light protection for the home, cottage, and students living away — without a full security team or forced cloud video. EyeNet confirms presence and alerts when an unknown person enters living areas.

Key Features:

  • Home protection — Intrusion and unknown-person alerts when the house is empty.
  • Family away / remote students — Peace of mind without cloud video.
  • Presence check — Confirm someone is (or should be) at home.
  • Secure zones — Alarm if an unspecified person enters living areas.
  • On-prem privacy — Footage stays local; phone alerts only when needed.

Target Users:

Families, parents, households, and students living away.

Available Modes:

Spot, Area
