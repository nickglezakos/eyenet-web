Application Deep Dive

Capabilities and target users for this story

Ships and industrial sites cannot lean on cloud VMS when links are thin. EyeNet keeps local truth about who is onboard or on site, packs reports for shore or HQ, and covers fall-critical zones so a fall becomes an immediate alert.

Key Features:

  • Industrial site zones — Restricted areas, yards, and production floors.
  • Fall detection — Decks, holds, warehouses, and industrial floors.
  • Shift & contractor presence — Who is on site without badge chasing.
  • Exact onboard count — Precise headcount at any moment.
  • Shore & HQ reports — Automated attendance packs to fleet HQ or plant control.
  • Fleet / site Matrix — VPN mesh; degraded mode — no data loss offline.

Target Users:

Maritime operators, fleet managers, shore HQ, plant / site ops, and industrial security.

Available Modes:

Gate, Area, Spot — aggregated via Matrix
