Application Deep Dive

Capabilities and target users for this story

EyeNet turns the unstaffed home into a care environment MDT teams and caregivers can trust. Falls, help-calls, presence, and zone breaches become targeted alerts — video stays on-prem. Partners fuse tokenized events into Virtual Ward workflows.

Key Features:

  • Caregiver presence — Right staff, right home, right time (visit assurance).
  • Secure zones — Alarm on unspecified persons in/around the dwelling.
  • Fall detection — Automation trigger → care team.
  • Voice / help-call — Spoken request for help → immediate actionable alert.
  • Clinical fuse — Tokenized events into ePokratis / IoMT / telemedicine (not cloud video).
  • Virtual Ward — Admission avoidance and early discharge at home.

Target Users:

Care providers, MDT clinicians, patients and families, Talos / TMAIX and eHealth partners.

Available Modes:

Spot, Area
