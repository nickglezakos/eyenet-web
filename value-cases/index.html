1 Know who is present and who is not

Home · office · warehouse · industrial · shipping · security — one flexible Presence tool

Presence is one flexible machine vision tool equally suitable for an intelligent doorbell at home, offices, warehouses, industrial floors, ships, and security posts. EyeNet shows who is in, where, and when — automatically — so teams stop chasing roll-calls and badge spreadsheets. Connectors with CRM systems, such as HubSpot, keep presence grants and contacts aligned as people move across sites.

  • Intelligent doorbell — who is at the door at home; presence without cloud video
  • Entrance / exit stations — Presence stations at doors for check-in / check-out
  • Vprofile cameras — offices, warehouses, industrial floors, and watch posts
  • Presence apps — staff mobile phones and desk PCs
  • Shipping & security posts — same stack for crew, shifts, and perimeter stations
  • Hands-off ops — purely automatic; no manual roll-call or chase
  • CRM Connectors — e.g. HubSpot CRM connector: sync contacts, post grants to HubSpot in real time
Automated notifications
  • Employee (Presence app confirmations)
  • Line manager (exceptions / late / no-show)
  • HR / People ops (frequent presence reports, near real time, daily, weekly, monthly, configurable, etc.)
  • Facilities · CRM contact timeline (grants)

Why it matters

One flexible machine vision Presence tool equally suitable for home, office, warehouse, industrial, shipping, and security — who is in, where, and when — without badges-only friction or spreadsheet attendance.

2 Screens that sell themselves

Retail · venues · agencies · brand studios · digital signage operators

Static screens burn budget because nobody can prove who saw what or what converted. EyeNet reads the room — age, gender, group size — and drives playlists from live audience signals in milliseconds. Views, dwell, and engagement turn concourses and spots into a measurable marketing studio instead of guesswork.

  • Read the room — age, gender, group size drive playlist switches in milliseconds
  • Zero surveys — know who sees each display: views, dwell, engagement
  • A/B in the wild — swap content on live audience data; measure what converts
  • Signage as studio — every screen becomes a measurable marketing surface across concourses, shops, and spots
  • Prove ROI — impression reports, engagement rates, footfall-to-sales funnels
Automated notifications
  • Marketing manager (campaign / ROI digests)
  • Content / media ops (playlist & A/B triggers)
  • Site / store operations
  • Agency · brand partner (scheduled reports)

3 See how shoppers move

Shop managers · store ops · multi-site retail

Shop managers need to know how customers move through the premises — not only that someone walked in. EyeNet turns existing cameras into people counts, paths, heatmaps, dwell, and occupancy, with behavioural alerts when zones get busy or quiet. Managers get digests and alerts; video never has to leave the store.

  • People counting — Face / Body / Both across entrances and floor cameras
  • Paths & heatmaps — how customers move through aisles and zones
  • Occupancy & peaks — busy hours by area, not guesswork
  • Dwell & visit patterns — where shoppers linger vs pass through
  • Behavioural alerts — occupancy / dwell / path / co-presence → mobile, email, webhook
  • On-prem privacy — video stays local; managers get digests and alerts, not cloud footage
Automated notifications
  • Shop manager (movement / occupancy digests)
  • Store ops · shift lead (busy-zone / dwell alerts)
  • Regional retail ops (multi-site packs)
  • Loss prevention · facilities (selected behavioural events)

Visual: eyenet-vision.com

Why it matters

Turn existing CCTV into floor intelligence — count, path, and act — without a separate analytics stack. Signage proves marketing ROI; shop floor proves how customers move.

4 Peace of mind at home — and away

Families · parents · households · students living away

Families need automated protection for the home, cottage, and students living away — without a full security team or forced cloud video. EyeNet confirms presence and alerts when an unknown person stands in front of a door or enters living areas. You get a few targeted phone notifications; footage stays local.

  • Home protection — intrusion and unknown-person alerts when the house is empty
  • Family away / remote students — peace of mind when loved ones live away, without cloud video
  • Presence check — confirm someone is (or should be) at home
  • Secure zones — alarm if an unspecified person stands in front of a door or enters living areas
  • On-prem privacy — footage stays local; phone alerts only when needed
Automated notifications
  • Parents / family (mobile push · email)
  • Resident (student / household member)
  • Caretaker / neighbour (optional)
  • Local response contact (critical only)

5 Hospital-grade care at home

Care provider · MDT clinician · patient / family · Talos / TMAIX eHealth partner

EyeNet turns the unstaffed home into a care environment that MDT teams and caregivers can trust. Falls, help-calls, presence, and zone breaches become a few targeted alerts in the clinical stack — video stays on-prem. Partners such as Talos / TMAIX fuse those events into Virtual Ward workflows without shipping footage to the cloud.

  • Caregiver presence — right staff, right home, right time (visit assurance)
  • Secure zones — alarm on unspecified persons in/around the dwelling
  • Fall detection — already-developed automation trigger → care team
  • Voice / help-call — spoken request for help → immediate actionable alert
  • Clinical fuse — tokenized events into ePokratis / IoMT / telemedicine (not cloud video)
  • Virtual Ward — hospital-level care at home: admission avoidance & early discharge
Automated notifications
  • MDT / care coordinator · on-call clinician
  • Assigned caregiver (mobile)
  • Patient family / sponsor (selected events)
  • Telemedicine / ePokratis case queue

Visual: talosgroup.eu/solutions/integration/homecare

Differentiator vs vitals-only RPM: the home becomes a monitored care environment. Privacy by architecture — vision stays on-prem; only fall / help / presence / zone events reach the health stack.

7 Who's onboard. Who's on site. Who fell.

Captain · fleet manager · shore HQ · plant / site ops · industrial security

Ships and industrial sites cannot lean on cloud VMS when links are thin or offline. EyeNet keeps local truth about who is onboard or on site and where they should be, then packs reports for shore or HQ. The same stack covers special zones that need fall detection — decks, holds, warehouses, and industrial floors — so a fall becomes an immediate alert, not a missed radio call.

  • Industrial site zones — restricted areas, yards, and production floors alert when the wrong people enter
  • Fall detection in special areas — decks, holds, warehouses, and industrial floors trigger fall alerts to bridge, shore, or site ops
  • Shift & contractor presence — know who is on site across shifts, contractors, and visitors without badge chasing
  • Hazard / PPE-aware alerts — optional triggers around high-risk zones and muster points on industrial campuses
  • Everyone in place at sea — Presence / Gate / Sentinel ensure crew are where they should be
  • Exact onboard count — know precisely how many people are onboard at any moment
  • Shore & HQ reports anytime — automated attendance & headcount packs to fleet HQ or plant control
  • Minimal connectivity — full local ops at sea or on remote industrial sites; sync over thin / intermittent links
  • Fleet / site Matrix — VPN mesh; degraded queue — no data loss when offline
Automated notifications
  • Captain · bridge / duty officer
  • Chief mate · security officer (onboard)
  • Shore fleet ops · CSO · plant / site supervisor (incl. fall alerts)
  • Fleet / site HR (attendance / compliance packs)

Visual: eyenet-vision.com

Why it matters

Ships and industrial sites cannot depend on cloud VMS. EyeNet gives captains, shore, and plant ops the same truth: who is onboard or on site, where they are — plus fall detection in the zones that need it — with reports that travel on minimal bandwidth.
