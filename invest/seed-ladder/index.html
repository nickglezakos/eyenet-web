Seed scenarios — from burn up

The seed burn floor is $500K — enough for about one year of product, launch, and working capital. Checks of $1M and $2.5M sit above that floor. Extra capital does not rewrite unit economics. It buys speed: more pilots, faster sales coverage, and an earlier geographic footprint.
$500K Burn Floor · Year-1 Ops

Personnel, marketing/CAC, tech, ops — lean runway.

$1M Base Seed

Burn covered, plus headroom for a fuller GTM.

$2.5M Accelerate Seed

Same economics, earlier scale and footprint.

Scenario Seed in What it buys Typical next round
Burn floor $500K Personnel ~$180K, marketing/CAC ~$135K, tech ~$125K, ops/R&D ~$60K. Launch, first pilots, lean runway. Series A / growth ~$700K–$1M after traction
Base $1M Burn covered, plus fuller GTM. Plan terms: ~25% at $3M pre ($4M post). Target: 5–10 pilots, $50–100K ARR, 50-lead pipeline, 2–3 partners. Series A $2–3M on $8–12M pre (Q3–Q4 2026)
Accelerate $2.5M Same core economics, earlier scale: denser sales, marketing, and multi-site footprint in the seed window. Dilution set by negotiated pre-money. Series A still available for land-and-expand; larger seed compresses time to that raise
Instrument

Convertible note or SAFE across scenarios. The $1M and $2.5M figures both appear in the business-plan set; treat them as capital choices on the same ladder, not as competing truths.
