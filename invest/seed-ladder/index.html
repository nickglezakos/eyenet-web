Seed scenarios — from burn up

The seed burn floor is $500K — enough for about one year of product, launch, and working capital. Checks of $1M and $2.5M sit above that floor. Extra capital does not rewrite unit economics. It buys speed and makes way for a sooner and bigger exit.

Personnel, marketing/CAC, tech, ops — lean runway.

$1M Base Seed

Burn covered, plus headroom for a fuller GTM.

Use of proceeds — speed

How capital above burn is allocated: spend buckets, scenario split, what we will not fund, and the speed KPIs that mark progress.

Burn first $500K year-1 ops

Personnel, CAC, tech, and ops covered. Launch and first pilots on lean runway.

Speed capital $1M / $2.5M

Only accelerate the same motions — sales, marketing, pilots, footprint — never rewrite CAC, LTV, or payback.

Exit logic Sooner · bigger

Denser proof earlier compresses the path to Series A and a larger exit window.
