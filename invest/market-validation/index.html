Market validation

FootfallCam is a successful, long-running commercial proof that buyers will pay for camera-based people analytics at scale. That is useful market validation for EyeNet — yet FootfallCam does not fully cover, and in several cases does not cover at all, EyeNet’s six vertical market segments. EyeNet’s wedge is sovereignty plus operational action across those six; FootfallCam’s wedge is retail and facility footfall BI.
Validation

FootfallCam shows a real paid market for edge people analytics — multi-year UK entity, partner channel, global installs.

Gap

Their primary industries (retail, malls, queues, washrooms, offices-as-occupancy) leave EyeNet’s six wedges largely open.

EyeNet six

HR Presence · Signage · Family / cottage · Home care · Night security · Shipping & industrial

1 EyeNet

On-prem edge vision + operational action

  • Presence, Gate, Signage, Vradar, Sentinel, Matrix
  • Detect → decide → act; video stays local by default
  • Any IP / Wi‑Fi / mobile camera on a ~16 GB PC; no per-camera licenses
  • Full B2C → B2B → Enterprise package ladder

2 FootfallCam

People-flow / space analytics platform

  • Hardware counters + V9 Analytic Manager
  • Measure visitors / occupancy / queues → BI and ops workflows
  • Own counters + Centroid on existing CCTV; UK-designed hardware + software
  • Contact / reseller sales — no self-serve B2C ladder

Gentle note — headcount vs financials

FootfallCam’s About page states a team of over 200 employees (HQ and regional offices), including 80+ engineers. That claim does not line up cleanly with the UK legal entity’s public accounts:

  • FOOTFALLCAM UK LIMITED reports only ~6 employees for most recent years (about 9 in the latest Endole extract for YE 2025).
  • Estimated UK-entity turnover peaks near $11M (YE 2023). At 200 FTEs that would imply only ~$55k revenue per employee — thin for a hardware + analytics vendor, and hard to fund if a large share of roles were UK-costed.
  • Filed UK staff costs for ~6 people fit a small UK core; they do not look like a 200-person payroll on that entity alone.

A fair reading is that 200+ / 80+ is likely a marketing or group-wide framing (possibly blending offshore teams, contractors, or partner capacity) rather than verified FTE on the UK books. Prefer the filed UK headcount for financial triangulation; treat the website staff figures as overstated or at least not directly comparable to the published P&L until a consolidated group disclosure appears.

What the filings support
  • Small UK entity headcount (~6–9) with meaningful net assets and cash
  • Ops narrative of UK + Malaysia centres — some delivery may sit outside the UK filing
  • Third-party aggregators sometimes show ~115–118 “employees” — still far below 200, and still not the UK statutory figure
