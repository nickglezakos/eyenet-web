Instant deployment — up and running in minutes, not months

Not another camera cloud — one platform that cuts across every buyer.

EyeNet is on-premises computer vision that turns ordinary cameras into vision nodes — they detect, decide, and act on site. Video stays local by default. One platform serves homes, care, offices, shops, security posts, ships, and plants.

Any camera Not vendor lock-in

EyeNet runs on any camera — existing cams, phones, PCs, or inexpensive ordinary cameras, with first detection in minutes on a ~16 GB PC. Not vendor lock-in.

Detect → decide → act Not passive VMS

EyeNet follows detect → decide → act. Detect uses any modern camera — including ones you already have — and turns them into intelligent machine-vision sensors at the edge that deliver live streams, recordings, and live automations. Decide is the automation rule engine. Act is the automation action side — alerts, webhooks, signage, gates, ERP/CRM, and more. Not passive VMS.

On-prem by default Sovereignty + offline

EyeNet stays on-prem by default — processing and footage stay on premises, with full operation when the internet is down, and essential updates still reach HQ even on a weak or limited connection. Live video can stream over VPN — a third-party VPN or EyeNet’s own — and remote nodes, including mobile cameras and EyeNet mobile apps, connect through EyeNet’s own mesh VPN.

No per-camera tax Hardware-bound scale

EyeNet scales without a per-camera tax — scale is the mesh you deploy, from one room to a campus, not a license ceiling or a GPU farm.

Zero / near-zero hardware Buy today

EyeNet starts with zero / near-zero hardware — staff use their own PC or mobile for presence grants; sites reuse cameras they already own, including thousands of employees without a camera fleet project.

Six wedges, one fabric Where it pays

EyeNet serves six wedges on one fabric — HR presence, adaptive signage, home and away, Virtual Ward, night / perimeter security, shipping and industrial — on one stack for who is here (Presence), alerts that act (Vradar), field / VIP watch (Sentinel), and many sites in one view (Matrix).
