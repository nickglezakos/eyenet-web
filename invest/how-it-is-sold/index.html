How it is sold

Three motions on one platform. Unit economics (CAC, LTV, payback, margins) are the core — they hold across seed capital scenarios.
Motion Price Economics Buyer
B2C Light $20–$300/mo · events $200–$1,200. Shelf: Signage Lite $30–100, Home Security Lite $20–50, Retail Compliance Lite $50–100, Event Security Lite $200–1,200/event. Free funnel 60–90 days, then Lite / Pro. Avg MRR ~$50 · CAC $20–30 · LTV:CAC ~30:1 · payback 1–2 mo Homeowners, micro-SMEs, consultants, small retail. Credit-card self-serve. Wants alerts and ROI without an IT project. Pockets: ~52K marketing consulting[1]; ~87M owner-occupied homes[2]; ~0.8M Airbnb hosts[3]; ~8K contract security[4]; ~1.0M+ retail + ~0.6M restaurants[5][6].
B2B Starter / Pro $500–$3,000/mo · ACV ~$12–36K. Starter $500–1,000 (1–5 sites, 5 cameras, one app). Professional $1,500–3,000 (5–20 sites, 25 cameras, multi-app). Mobile / wearable +40–60%. Retention 85–90% · CAC payback ~3–4 mo Multi-site retail, offices, regional security, campuses, warehouses. Buys Presence, Gate, and Signage on ROI, not a custom build. Pockets: ~8K contract security (Starter → Pro)[4]; ~99K public K–12[7]; ~38K warehousing[8].
Enterprise $5,000+/mo · contracts $6K–$150K+/yr. Unlimited sites and cameras, Matrix, VPN fleets, custom ERP / CRM / eHealth / SIEM. Modeled LTV ~$144K · CAC $3–5K · LTV:CAC ~40:1 Home care, maritime, industrial, municipal, multi-brand retail. On-prem, data residency, partners.
