The bet

Incumbents sell cloud storage, bandwidth, and recordings. EyeNet sells sovereignty and operational action: any camera becomes a vision node on a ~16 GB PC, first detection in minutes, no per-camera licenses, and video that never has to leave the site. Scale is hardware-bound — one room to a campus — with no technical ceiling.

02

What changes

Users are not locked to a camera wall. The platform watches 24/7; the right person gets a targeted alert — mobile, email, webhook, clinical app, or HubSpot — only when something needs them. Ordinary cameras become an always-on assistant that decides and acts on premises, including when the internet is down.
Edge Not cloud-bound

On-device AI. Full offline. Data sovereignty by default. Processing and storage stay on premises.

Action Not passive VMS

Triggers fire mobile, email, webhooks, signage, and gates. Each camera has its own rules.

Scale Hardware-bound

Any IP, WiFi, or phone camera. ~16 GB PC. One room to an enterprise mesh. No GPU farm.

Incumbents sell

  • Cloud storage & monthly bandwidth
  • Passive recordings & dashboards
  • Generic “AI boxes”
  • Projects that take quarters

EyeNet ships

  • On-prem decisions & zero video cloud
  • Targeted notifications that run the operation
  • Purpose-built apps for real jobs
  • Detection in minutes — scale by hardware

How it multiplies operator capacity

  • Vision nodes — face, count, demographics, and routes on cameras the site already owns.
  • Entrances, restricted zones, VIP watchlists — headcount, unauthorised presence, and person-of-interest alerts without extra posts.
  • Signage that reads the room — age, gender, and group size switch content in milliseconds, with impressions and dwell.
  • Remote control from a phone — VPN access to cameras, triggers, and dashboards. Footage stays on premises even when the operator does not.

The seed burn floor is $500K — enough for about one year of product, launch, and working capital. Checks of $1M and $2.5M sit above that floor. Extra capital does not rewrite unit economics. It buys speed: more pilots, faster sales coverage, and an earlier geographic footprint.

Gentle note — headcount vs financials

FootfallCam’s About page states a team of over 200 employees (HQ and regional offices), including 80+ engineers. That claim does not line up cleanly with the UK legal entity’s public accounts:

  • FOOTFALLCAM UK LIMITED reports only ~6 employees for most recent years (about 9 in the latest Endole extract for YE 2025).
  • Estimated UK-entity turnover peaks near $11M (YE 2023). At 200 FTEs that would imply only ~$55k revenue per employee — thin for a hardware + analytics vendor, and hard to fund if a large share of roles were UK-costed.
  • Filed UK staff costs for ~6 people fit a small UK core; they do not look like a 200-person payroll on that entity alone.

A fair reading is that 200+ / 80+ is likely a marketing or group-wide framing (possibly blending offshore teams, contractors, or partner capacity) rather than verified FTE on the UK books. Prefer the filed UK headcount for financial triangulation; treat the website staff figures as overstated or at least not directly comparable to the published P&L until a consolidated group disclosure appears.

Project status

Product readiness and partners from the July 2026 business-plan primer, plus the capital and growth path restated in the same seed-ladder language as sections 05–06. Team started July 2024; working prototype in under six months. Five of six products are beta-ready.

The six products

Product Job
EyeNet Presence Automatic attendance with face + QR/NFC check-in. Replaces paper timesheets and badge swipes.
EyeNet Signage Digital screens that change content based on who is watching (age, gender). Measurable advertising.
EyeNet Vradar Alert brain: rules such as crowd thresholds that fire mobile, email, or other actions. Powers the rest of the stack.
EyeNet Gate Entrance and exit monitoring. Counts people in and out; flags suspicious faces in real time.
EyeNet Sentinel Wearable camera glasses + drones for VIP / bodyguard teams. Spots persons of interest in a crowd.
EyeNet Matrix One dashboard across dozens of sites. Franchise chains, shipping fleets, multi-location businesses.

Status: five of six products beta-ready and with design partners for real-world testing (Q3 2026).
