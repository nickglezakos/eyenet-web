01

What is EyeNet

EyeNet is on-premises computer vision that turns ordinary cameras into vision nodes — they detect, decide, and act on site. Video stays local by default. One platform serves homes, care, offices, shops, security posts, ships, and plants.

Who buys — and why now

Real buyers, real jobs: each persona hits a wall with incumbents — then starts on cameras they already have and gets the outcome today.

An HR manager can now let every employee check in from their own PC or phone — and push grants to the ERP/CRM — with zero new hardware, even at thousands of seats.

A shop or mall owner can now prove who is watching a screen and switch the playlist on cams they already have, without a locked player kit.

A parent can now get private intrusion alerts on the home cams they already own — or a cheap everyday camera — with no cloud subscription.

A parent of a student living away can now watch a remote flat or cottage with one ordinary cam — phone pings only, no guard contract or cloud VMS.

A care provider can now turn a patient room cam into fall and help-call events for the clinical stack — without shipping cloud video.

A security ops lead can now get night vigilance and perimeter from the fence and floor cams already there — without per-camera seats or another night shift.

A captain or plant ops lead can now know who’s onboard and who’s in zone when the link dies — on the cams already aboard or on site.

Powerful features

Platform reasons that cut across every persona — why EyeNet is not another camera cloud.

Any camera Not vendor lock-in

Existing cams, phones, PCs — or inexpensive ordinary cameras. First detection in minutes on a ~16 GB PC.

Detect → decide → act Not passive VMS

Targeted alerts to mobile, email, webhooks, signage, gates, and the ERP/CRM — only when something needs a person.

On-prem by default Sovereignty + offline

Processing and footage stay on premises. Full operation when the internet is down; Matrix syncs on thin links.

No per-camera tax Hardware-bound scale

Scale is the mesh you deploy — one room to a campus — not a per-camera license ceiling or a GPU farm.

Zero / near-zero hardware Buy today

Staff use their own PC or mobile for presence grants; sites reuse cameras they already own. EyeNet scales to thousands of employees without a camera fleet project.

What changes

Users are not locked to a camera wall. The platform watches 24/7; the right person gets a targeted alert — mobile, email, webhook, clinical app, or HubSpot — only when something needs them. Ordinary cameras become an always-on assistant that decides and acts on premises, including when the internet is down.

Edge Not cloud-bound

On-device AI. Full offline. Data sovereignty by default. Processing and storage stay on premises.

Action Not passive VMS

Triggers fire mobile, email, webhooks, signage, and gates. Each camera has its own rules.

Scale Hardware-bound

Any IP, WiFi, or phone camera. ~16 GB PC. One room to an enterprise mesh. No GPU farm.

Incumbents sell

  • Cloud storage & monthly bandwidth
  • Passive recordings & dashboards
  • Generic “AI boxes”
  • Projects that take quarters

EyeNet ships

  • On-prem decisions & zero video cloud
  • Targeted notifications that run the operation
  • Purpose-built apps for real jobs
  • Detection in minutes — scale by hardware

How it multiplies operator capacity

  • Vision nodes — face, count, demographics, and routes on cameras the site already owns.
  • Entrances, restricted zones, VIP watchlists — headcount, unauthorised presence, and person-of-interest alerts without extra posts.
  • Signage that reads the room — age, gender, and group size switch content in milliseconds, with impressions and dwell.
  • Remote control from a phone — VPN access to cameras, triggers, and dashboards. Footage stays on premises even when the operator does not.
