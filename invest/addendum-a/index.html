Project status

Product readiness and partners from the July 2026 business-plan primer, plus the capital and growth path restated in the same seed-ladder language as sections 05–06. Team started July 2024; working prototype in under six months. Five of six products are beta-ready.

The six products

Product Job
EyeNet Presence Automatic attendance with face + QR/NFC check-in. Replaces paper timesheets and badge swipes.
EyeNet Signage Digital screens that change content based on who is watching (age, gender). Measurable advertising.
EyeNet Vradar Alert brain: rules such as crowd thresholds that fire mobile, email, or other actions. Powers the rest of the stack.
EyeNet Gate Entrance and exit monitoring. Counts people in and out; flags suspicious faces in real time.
EyeNet Sentinel Wearable camera glasses + drones for VIP / bodyguard teams. Spots persons of interest in a crowd.
EyeNet Matrix One dashboard across dozens of sites. Franchise chains, shipping fleets, multi-location businesses.

Status: five of six products beta-ready and with design partners for real-world testing (Q3 2026).

Current milestones

  • July 2024: Working prototype delivered in under six months.
  • Q3 2026: Five of six products beta-ready and in the hands of design partners.
  • Two distribution partners signed: TMA (EyeNet-branded) and NewGen Group (white-label). Both early-stage, pursuing hot leads across municipalities, enterprises, and maritime clients.
  • Deloitte engagement: NewGen has laid groundwork for a demo with Deloitte Greece; presentation to the Deloitte EMEA Cyber Security Committee tentatively scheduled for September 2026.
  • LG International hot lead: TMA is positioning EyeNet for a European municipality and has identified a high-potential early-stage lead with LG International — major upside if it converts.

Capital and growth path

Same seed ladder as section 05: burn floor first, then how much speed to buy. Extra capital does not rewrite unit economics — it buys earlier pilots, denser sales, and the footprint steps in section 06. Instrument: convertible note or SAFE across scenarios.

Horizon Plan
Seed · Burn floor · $500K Year-1 ops floor: launch, first pilots, lean runway. Personnel / marketing / tech / ops covered; Greek unit as EU pilot. Typical next: Series A / growth ~$700K–$1M after traction.
Seed · Base · $1M Burn covered, plus fuller GTM. Plan terms: ~25% at $3M pre ($4M post). Target: 5–10 pilots, $50–100K ARR, 50-lead pipeline, 2–3 partners; formal Greek + U.S. units. Typical next: Series A $2–3M on $8–12M pre (Q3–Q4 2026).
Seed · Accelerate · $2.5M Same core economics, earlier scale: denser sales and multi-site footprint in the seed window; Greek + U.S. units and London representation. Series A still available for land-and-expand; larger seed compresses time to that raise.
Series A Size and timing scale with seed choice: ~$700K–$1M after burn-floor traction; $2–3M on the base path; larger seed still leaves Series A for land-and-expand. Use of proceeds: dedicated sales, marketing ramp, path toward ~200 customers.
2027 ~$1M revenue · 50 B2B · ~600 consumer users — first commercial proof after seed launch of the five beta-ready products.
2028 ~$8M revenue · 200 B2B · 6,000+ consumer — breakeven. Capital above burn accelerates arrival; unit economics stay the same.
2029 ~$26M revenue · 500 B2B · 20,000+ consumer — solidly profitable on the three motions (B2C Light, B2B Starter / Pro, Enterprise).
2030–2031 Exit path: IPO or strategic acquisition at $500M–$1B (plan target range).

Why this path is underwritable

  • Products are built, not imagined: Five of six apps exist as working beta. Capital on the seed ladder buys selling what works — computer vision, recognition, and alert automation are already done — not building from scratch.
  • Distribution is already on board: TMA and NewGen are signed partners pursuing leads, not letters of intent. That de-risks the first ~50 paying customers and the base-seed pipeline target.
  • Deloitte is a force multiplier in waiting: EMEA Cyber Security Committee presentation tentatively on the calendar for September 2026.
  • LG International is a global-scale multiplier: Early-stage hot lead, not a closed deal. A win with LG’s trading and investment arm can unlock manufacturing, logistics, retail, and government supply-chain distribution and open other Korean / Asian conglomerate doors.
  • Burn floor first, then speed: Underwrite $500K ops, then choose $1M or $2.5M for how fast footprint and GTM stand up. CAC, LTV, and payback in How it is sold hold across all three seed scenarios.
