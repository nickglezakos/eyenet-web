Where it pays

Six wedges. Same platform. The market others abandon is connectivity- and privacy-hostile: ships, industrial sites, clinics, family homes, night posts, Virtual Wards.
Wedge Buyer Why it is paid for
HR presence HR, facilities, office ops Automatic staff presence on-site and remote, with zero manual roll-call. HubSpot is the first CRM connector.
Vision-adaptive intelligent signage Retail, venues, agencies, brand studios Age, gender, and group size switch playlists in milliseconds. Impressions, dwell, and A/B turn screens into a measurable studio.
Family protection at home and remote Families, parents, students living away Intrusion, unknown-person, and presence alerts for the home and for people living away. Footage stays local.
Home care / Virtual Ward Care provider, clinician, Talos / TMAIX Caregiver presence, fall, help-call, and zone events into the health stack — tokenized, not cloud video. Safety layer vitals-only RPM cannot fake.
Security: night and perimeter Security firms, corporate & industrial sites Guard vigilance, perimeter Presence, Vradar motion, and forensics. Night coverage stops scaling 1:1 with sites.
Shipping and industrial Captain, fleet manager, plant & site ops Who is onboard or on site, restricted zones, and headcount packs. Full local ops; sync on thin links. Matrix when offline.
