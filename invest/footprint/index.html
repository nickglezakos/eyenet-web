Footprint steps by scenario

Organizational build-out scales with capital above burn. Unit economics stay the same; the company simply stands up earlier.
Step Burn $500K Base $1M Accelerate $2.5M
1 · Greek unit Yes — EU pilot, GDPR-first, lean team Yes — formal Greek business unit Yes — Greek unit as EU operating base
2 · U.S. business unit Lean commercial start in primary market Yes — U.S. unit for enterprise + B2C launch Yes — U.S. unit with denser sales and partner coverage
3 · London office — — Yes — London representation for UK / North-West Europe

Greece is the plan’s EU pilot (compliance, education, tourism). The United States is the primary commercial market. London appears only on the high seed: a representation office, not a second R&D center.

What is hard to copy

Privacy-first edge computer vision, six vertical cases on one platform — HR, signage, family protection, home care, night and perimeter security, shipping and industrial — plus partner channels: Talos / TMAIX in eHealth, maritime and plant ops, HubSpot-first CRM. Presence, Vradar, Sentinel, and Matrix are one fabric. A feature checkbox does not reproduce that stack.

What to underwrite

Burn floor first, then how much speed to buy. CAC, LTV, and payback are the core. Capital above $500K accelerates the same motions — consumer volume, mid-market land-and-expand, enterprise ACV — and the footprint steps above.
