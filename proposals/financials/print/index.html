Cash & runway

Plan cash model: Seed $2.5M funds operations until Accelerate ($5–7M, mid $6M) closes; combined ~$8.5M covers the investment phase through profitability. Unit economics stay the same.

Verdict

Under the baseline plan the company does not operate into a zero-cash gap: Seed bridge (~6–12 months) is sized to reach Accelerate, and Accelerate replenishes cash with an ~18-month working-capital runway toward profitability. Missing Accelerate on time is the main cash-risk failure mode.

7.2 Seed-period burn (derived from this pack’s FTE)

People cost on the seed roster is ~$1.265M/yr ≈ ~$105K/month. With marketing/tech/ops non-people programs, an all-in seed burn on the order of ~$150–250K/month (mid ~$200K) is the planning band. At ~$200K/mo, $2.5M alone ≈ ~12 months of runway — enough to cover Q1–Q2 2026 seed spend and still leave months of cushion into the Accelerate window (Q3–Q4 2026).

Underwriting checklist & failure modes

Five holds for diligence, the use-of-proceeds playbook, and what breaks the financials even when activity looks busy.

01 · Price from the shelf

B2C Lite SKUs · B2B Starter/Pro bands · Enterprise $5K+/mo floor

02 · Unit economics hold

CAC / LTV / payback unchanged from Seed $2.5M to Accelerate $5–7M

03 · Seed proof first

$50–100K ARR · 5–10 pilots · 50 leads · 2–3 partners

04 · Scale years

$1M / $8M / $26M with 50→200→500 B2B and 600→6K→20K B2C

05 · Contract quality rises

Blended B2B ACV ~$13K → $22K → $28K with 85–90% retention · TAM share as ceiling context

Use-of-proceeds underwriting playbook

  1. Underwrite Seed $2.5M with the five-bucket split → ~13.7 FTE + ~$1.23M non-people.
  2. Then Accelerate $5–7M (Series A; mid often ~$6M on $20–30M pre) on the same metric frame.
  3. Hold unit economics constant across Seed and Accelerate; measure speed KPIs, not a new CAC story.
  4. Price seed headcount with §05 rates ($82 / $76 / $109 / $83 / $100K); do not treat Marketing or Tech as 100% people.
  5. Compare rounds in §03 — marketing, sales/GTM, tech/product, ops/runway, total raise.

Failure modes that break the financials

  • Lots of pilots but ARR < $50K in-seed
  • 50 logos but ACV stuck ≪ $12K (cannot make $1M with 600 B2C alone)
  • Hit logo counts but churn > 10–15% (never reach 200/500 stock)

Bottom line: rising B2B book 50 → 200 → 500 with rising ACV ~$13K → $22K → $28K, plus B2C 600 → 6K → 20K at ~$50 MRR — gated by 5–10 pilots and $50–100K ARR in the $2.5M seed. Accelerate ($5–7M) buys scale on the same economics story.

Financial jargon in plain English

For non-financial readers. Skim the cheat sheet; use definitions when a term appears in a table.

Cheat sheet

Revenue

Money customers pay you. Plan ~$1M → $8M → $26M.

ARR

Yearly software run-rate if today’s subscriptions kept going for a year. Seed proof $50–100K ARR.

MRR

Same idea per month. ARR ≈ MRR × 12. B2C avg ~$50 MRR.

ACV

Typical yearly value of one business contract. B2B ~$12–36K; mix rises ~$13K → $22K → $28K.

CAC

Spend to win one paying customer. B2C $20–30; Enterprise $3–5K.

LTV

Total money expected from one customer over their life. Enterprise ~$144K.

LTV:CAC

Dollars back per dollar spent to acquire (e.g. 30:1).

Payback

Months until payments cover CAC. B2C 1–2 mo; B2B 3–4 mo.

Retention / churn

Share who stay / leave. B2B plan 85–90% retention.

TAM

Biggest market you could theoretically sell into. B2C $0.5–1.5B; B2B/Ent $2–4B.

Seed

This plan’s seed ask is $2.5M for launch, pilots, and footprint.

Use of proceeds

How invested money will be spent.

Speed / scale

Accelerate scales GTM faster on the same unit economics — not a new model.

SAFE / note

Cash in now; exact ownership % set later.

Pre- / post-money

Company value before / after new cash. Accelerate framed at $20–30M pre.

Dilution

Founders’ % ownership shrinks when new investors buy in.

FTE

One full-time person’s year of work.

Logo

One named customer account.

Pipeline

List of potential deals being worked. Seed asks 50-lead pipeline.

Land-and-expand

Start small, then sell more later — how ACV rises.

Freemium

Free 60–90 days, then Lite / Pro.

GTM

Go-to-market — how you sell and market.

Underwrite

Investor judgment: “Can I trust these numbers?”

Unit economics

Whether one customer is profitable on paper (CAC vs LTV).

Runway

How many months you can keep operating with cash on hand before needing more capital or profits.

Breakeven

Business stops losing money overall (~2028 on plan).

Accelerate

Name for Series A in this plan: $5–7M on $20–30M pre (often ~$6M) after the $2.5M seed.

Footprint

Where the company is set up (Greece, U.S., London).

Pilot

First real-world trial — often limited — before a full contract.

ARR vs ACV vs MRR

Quick distinction

MRR = monthly for one/many subscriptions. ACV = yearly value of one B2B deal. ARR = yearly run-rate across the book. If 50 customers average $12.8K ACV → ~$640K of that book’s ARR.

Tiny examples with EyeNet numbers

  • ARR vs one contract: Starter ~$750/mo → ~$9K ACV. Ten such ≈ ~$90K ARR — inside seed $50–100K ARR gate.
  • CAC payback: Spend $25 to win B2C at $50/mo → payback about half a month to one month (plan 1–2 mo with friction).
  • LTV:CAC: Spend $30 CAC, get ~$900 LTV → 30:1.
  • Retention math: Start with 200 B2B at 90% retention → keep 180; must add new logos to end higher.
  • Accelerate pre/post: $20–30M pre + ~$6M mid → roughly 20–25% dilution at that round.
