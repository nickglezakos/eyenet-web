Case 08

Perimeters that detect, verify, and dispatch

Corporate campuses · industrial sites · defence / critical infrastructure

Wide-area and virtual perimeters cannot depend on a wall of feeds or a fence that does not exist. EyeNet is the on-premises software brain for enterprise security: it turns Gate, Area, Presence, Vradar, Sentinel, and Drone-mode feeds into verified alarms, then dispatches response — with Matrix backup viewing and local archives — without proprietary camera lock-in. Towers, solar masts, UAVs, PA, and radio remain partner or site-supplied; EyeNet ingests their video and runs Detect → Decide → Dispatch.

  • Virtual perimeters — Presence and Area define restricted zones across campuses, plants, and open industrial ground where there is no physical fence
  • Gate + Area detection — approaches, site boundaries, and critical corridors on fixed, mobile, and tower cameras
  • Vradar alarm rules — intrusion, loitering, after-hours, and multi-condition triggers that cut false walls of video
  • Drone / wearable response — Drone mode and Goggles analyse aerial and body-worn streams; webhooks cue partner UAV docks when aviation kit is ready
  • Sentinel while posts remain — keep static posts honest during the shift to mobile, alarm-led response
  • Matrix + VPN mesh — control room and backup SOC share one installation mesh; degraded mode keeps reachable cameras visible
  • On-prem forensics — continuous / event recording and archives sized to load — evidence stays local, not in a cloud VMS
  • Partner hardware welcome — PTZ/thermal towers, solar mobiles, PA talk-down, radio/AVL integrate; EyeNet stays the AI detection and alarm layer

Why it matters

Enterprise and defence sites need alarm-led operations — not operators glued to a video wall. EyeNet detects on the edge, decides with Vradar and Presence rules, and dispatches response assets while Matrix keeps hub and backup desks aligned on-premises.
