Case 01

Know who is present and who is not

Home · office · warehouse · industrial · shipping · security — one flexible Presence tool

Presence is one flexible machine vision tool equally suitable for an intelligent doorbell at home, offices, warehouses, industrial floors, ships, and security posts. EyeNet shows who is in, where, and when — automatically — so teams stop chasing roll-calls and badge spreadsheets.
