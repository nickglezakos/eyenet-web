EyeNet case 05: Hospital-grade care at home · Closest competitor: SafelyYou

Competitor story

Senior-living operators reducing falls and ER visits with room cameras + AI.

SafelyYou leads camera-based fall detection and AI care tools for senior living (Respond, Clarity, Aware), pairing vision with remote clinical workflows. It is the closest rival for hospital-grade care monitoring. The stack is care-vertical and typically appliance/cloud oriented — not EyeNet’s cross-industry on-prem action platform.

Where they stop: Excellent care vertical depth — not a single brain for mall, maritime, perimeter, and city cases.
