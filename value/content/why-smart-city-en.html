Case 09

City cameras that decide and act

Municipal ops · traffic / public safety · multi-borough camera meshes

Smart cities drown in passive feeds when cameras are scattered across boroughs and nobody can act on what they see. EyeNet enrols those cams into one on-premises / VPN fabric, runs the full trigger and automation stack — thresholds, dwell, direction, schedules, multi-condition Vradar rules — and keeps forensics in the recording pipeline and media services. Matrix links district hubs so the city runs on decisions and actions, not a wall of screens.

  • Citywide camera mesh — IP, Wi‑Fi, and soft cams across junctions, plazas, depots, and civic buildings in one fabric
  • Full trigger / automation rules — per-camera Vradar conditions (counts, dwell, velocity, direction, time-of-day, AND/OR stacks) including the latest action chains
  • Multi-channel actions — mobile push, email, webhooks, signage, access / lighting partners — without shipping video to the cloud
  • Cross-zone correlation — multi-camera triggers for paths across districts and corridors
  • Recording pipeline — continuous and event archives sized for municipal retention policies
  • Media services forensics — collections, clip export, audit trails for investigations — evidence stays local
  • Matrix multi-site — hub + satellite ops centres share viewing; degraded queues protect truth when a borough link drops
  • On-prem sovereignty — privacy by architecture for public-space vision; remote VPN only when policy allows

Why it matters

Scattered city cameras only pay when they decide and act. EyeNet turns municipal estates into trigger-led operations — with Matrix linking districts and forensics from recording and media services that never need a cloud VMS mandate.
