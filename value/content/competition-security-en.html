Competitor story

Retail, construction, and site owners replacing or augmenting night guards with mobile towers.

LiveView Technologies (LVT) sells rapidly deployable mobile security units with cameras, deterrence, and remote monitoring — a hardware+cloud answer to “eyes on site when posts are expensive.” It competes with EyeNet’s night-vigilance narrative via physical units and monitoring services, not via any-camera software that also runs home, care, and city cases.

Where they stop: Mobile tower + monitoring estate — not software-only any-camera on-prem across EyeNet cases.
