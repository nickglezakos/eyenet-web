EyeNet case 01: Know who is present and who is not · Closest competitor: Verkada

Competitor story

Workplace security & facilities teams buying cloud cameras with people analytics.

Verkada sells an integrated cloud physical-security platform — cameras, access, sensors, and People Analytics / occupancy — to enterprises that want dashboards without managing a VMS. It is strong at workplace presence and multi-site admin when buyers accept Verkada hardware and a cloud control plane. It does not position as an any-camera, on-prem Detect → Decide → Act stack for shipping, home care, city ops, and the rest of EyeNet’s cases.

Where they stop: Cloud + proprietary device estate; presence analytics for workplaces — not a sovereign multi-vertical action fabric.
