EyeNet case 04: Peace of mind at home — and away · Closest competitor: Ring (Amazon)

Competitor story

Consumers and small properties wanting doorbell / outdoor cams with app alerts.

Ring (Amazon) owns the consumer home-security camera and doorbell category — app-first alerts, neighborhood features, and Amazon retail distribution. It is the closest mass-market rival for home/away peace of mind. The model is cloud consumer devices and subscriptions, not an enterprise-grade any-camera on-prem fabric that also serves industrial and city buyers.

Where they stop: Consumer cloud cameras — not B2B/enterprise multi-vertical Detect → Decide → Act on-prem.
