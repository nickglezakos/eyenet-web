Competitor story

Critical infrastructure and industrial sites buying perimeter intrusion detection.

Senstar (NASDAQ: SNT) provides perimeter intrusion detection, video, and access products for campuses, utilities, and industrial sites — virtual and physical perimeter sensing with a long security heritage. It is a natural rival for Detect → Dispatch on fences and zones. It does not claim EyeNet’s consumer-to-city software breadth on any camera without a perimeter-product SKU mindset.

Where they stop: Strong perimeter product company — not a multi-vertical on-prem action OS for all EyeNet cases.
