EyeNet Vision — Project Snapshot

July 2026 · eyenet-vision.com

EyeNet Vision is AI software that turns regular cameras (such as ip, wifi, security, mobile embedded, IOT, etc) into smart cameras — running entirely on a standard PC with no cloud dependency, no expensive GPU servers, and all video kept on-premises. It handles people counting, facial recognition, age/gender detection, and automated actions (alerts, digital signage, access control, attendance logging).

Key Highlights

  • Six products on one platform: Presence (attendance), Signage (smart screens), Vradar (alert rules engine), Gate (entrance monitoring), Sentinel (VIP bodyguard wearables/drones), Matrix (multi-site dashboard).
    Same underlying AI engine, packaged for different use cases — customers can start with one module and add more later.
    All six share a unified microservices architecture, so features built for one product benefit the entire platform.
  • 5 of 6 products beta-ready today, working prototype delivered within 6 months (July 2024).
    The core computer vision, facial recognition, and alert automation engines are fully functional and tested.
    Seed capital goes to sales, marketing, and customer onboarding — not to finishing half-built software.
  • Two distribution partners signed: TMA and NewGen Group, both pursuing early-stage enterprise & maritime leads.
    TMA is positioning EyeNet for a European municipality and has identified a hot lead with LG International.
    NewGen Group has laid the groundwork for a demo with Deloitte Greece, targeting enterprise and government buyers.
  • Deloitte & LG International pipeline: Enterprise validation already in motion.
    Deloitte EMEA Cyber Security Committee presentation tentatively scheduled for September 2026 — a powerful reference in waiting.
    LG International (LG's trading and investment arm) represents a global-scale opportunity across manufacturing, logistics, retail, and government supply chains worldwide.
  • Raising $2.5M Seed (Q3–Q4 2026) → $5–7M Series A → target ~$1M revenue (2027) → ~$26M (2029) → $500M–$1B exit by 2030–2031.
    Enterprise contracts average $12,000/month with 80–85% gross margins; consumer subscriptions add volume at $20–30/month.
    Breakeven projected by 2028, with the development team's burn rate covered by Q1 2027 from existing leads alone.
  • Privacy-first, runs on-prem: All video stays local, works offline, runs on any standard PC with 16 GB RAM.
    No cloud dependency, no expensive GPU servers, no internet required for core operation — internet is only needed for remote phone notifications.
    Infinitely scalable microservices architecture: from a single camera in a small warehouse to hundreds of cameras across enterprise campuses, all managed from one central dashboard.
