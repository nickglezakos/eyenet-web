Overview

EyeNet Vision is an intelligent vision technology platform delivering six specialized applications across security, marketing, compliance, and operations verticals. Built on the EyeNet platform, it combines proprietary computer vision, demographic analysis, and event-driven automation to transform passive surveillance into active, responsive operational intelligence.

Core Value Proposition

Privacy-first, on-premises vision analytics with real-time automated actions — no cloud dependency, full offline capability, infinitely scalable from single camera to enterprise-wide deployment.

Services & Verticals

About EyeNet

On-premises, edge-computing computer vision platform that transforms standard cameras into intelligent machine vision nodes — no GPU servers, no cloud subscriptions, no proprietary hardware lock-in. Runs on standard business hardware (16 GB RAM, modern multi-core CPU); connect any IP camera, WiFi cam, or mobile phone and the first detection runs in minutes. Scales from a single lens to facility-wide mesh, from one office to entire maritime fleets, all connected securely over VPN with zero cloud dependency for video or data.

Use Cases

  • Staff Presence & Attendance — QR/NFC + facial recognition dual-factor verification with automated reporting (Presence, Gate)
  • Audience-Aware Digital Signage — Displays adapt content in milliseconds based on viewer age, gender, group size (Signage)
  • Entrance & Gate Monitoring — Real-time people counters, demographics, heatmaps, facial recognition at all entry points (Gate, Presence)
  • Restricted Area Protection — AI-enforced virtual perimeters with instant unauthorised presence alerts and lone-worker monitoring (Vradar, Presence)
  • VIP & Personnel Protection — On-premises face recognition with POI alerts streamed to eyewear and mobile devices (Sentinel, Vradar)
  • Remote Operational Control — VPN-powered access to every camera, trigger, and dashboard worldwide from a single phone (Matrix, Gate)
  • Maritime & Fleet Compliance — Offline-capable staff presence tracking across entire fleets with real-time incident alerts (Presence, Matrix)
  • Multi-Site Aggregated Reporting — Consolidated dashboards with degraded mode when sites are unreachable (Matrix)

Core Platform Functionalities

  • Computer Vision & People Analytics: Real-time tracking, counting, 3D route analysis, demographic detection (age, gender)
  • Camera & Hardware Integration: IP cameras, WiFi cams, soft cameras via mobile apps, full camera health monitoring
  • Intelligent Triggers & Automated Actions: Per-camera configurable triggers with AND/OR logic; multi-channel actions (push, email, SMS, signage switching, gate control, webhooks)
  • Privacy-First Video & Data Management: Local storage, no cloud required, full offline operation — internet needed only for remote notifications
  • Multi-Channel Notifications: Mobile push, in-app alerts, email, SMS, webhook integrations with delivery audit logging
  • Analytics & Insights: Real-time dashboards, historical trends, behavioral pattern recognition, trigger performance stats
  • Enterprise-Grade Security: Role-based access control, file-level encryption, optional private VPN, complete data sovereignty
  • Scalability & Extensibility: Scales from home to industrial sites; REST APIs, webhooks, modular plugin architecture, unlimited cameras
