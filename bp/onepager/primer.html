EyeNet Vision — Business Plan Primer

July 2026 · eyenet-vision.com

EyeNet Vision is software that turns regular security cameras into smart cameras. Point a camera at a door, a shop floor, or a restricted hallway — and EyeNet can count people, recognise faces, figure out age and gender, and then automatically do something about it: send an alert, switch a digital sign, lock a gate, or log an attendance report. It all runs on a normal desktop PC (no expensive GPU servers, no cloud subscriptions needed) and keeps every bit of video on your own premises. Think of it as brains for cameras — six different products for six different jobs.

The team started in July 2024 and had a working prototype in six months. Today, five of the six products are ready to launch. We're raising $2.5 million right now (Seed round) to finish validation and sign the first paying customers, followed by a $5–7 million Series A to scale the sales team and go to market. If the plan works, EyeNet grows from roughly $1 million in annual revenue to $26 million by 2029, with a target exit — either an IPO or a sale to a bigger company — at a valuation of $500 million to $1 billion by 2030–2031.

At a Glance

The Six Products

  • Eyenet Presence — Automatic attendance tracking with face + QR/NFC check-in. Replaces paper timesheets and badge swipes.
  • Eyenet Signage — Digital screens that change what they show based on who's watching (age, gender). Smarter advertising.
  • Eyenet Vradar — The alert brain: set rules like "if more than 50 people crowd this area, text me." Powers everything else.
  • Eyenet Gate — Entrance and exit monitoring. Counts everyone coming and going, flags suspicious faces in real time.
  • Eyenet Sentinel — Wearable camera glasses + drones for VIP bodyguard teams. Spots persons of interest in a crowd.
  • Eyenet Matrix — One dashboard to control dozens of sites. Built for franchise chains, shipping fleets, and multi-location businesses.

What Makes EyeNet Different

  • Privacy-first: All video stays on your own hardware. Nothing goes to the cloud. No internet needed to operate.
  • No expensive hardware: Runs on a standard PC with 16 GB of RAM. Works with any IP camera, WiFi cam, or even a mobile phone.
  • Low barrier to entry: Because EyeNet runs on everyday hardware — any standard PC with 16 GB of RAM and a modern CPU — there's no need for specialised IT infrastructure, expensive GPU servers, or proprietary appliances. A small retail shop, a school, a factory floor, or a government building can all get up and running with hardware they already own.
  • Infinitely scalable: EyeNet's microservices architecture and Matrix multi-site functionality mean the platform scales seamlessly from a single camera in a small warehouse to hundreds of cameras across entire industrial installations, maritime fleets, or enterprise campuses — all managed from one central dashboard over a secure VPN connection, with no architectural ceiling on deployment size.
  • Works offline: Internet is only needed if you want remote phone notifications. Core operation keeps running even if the connection drops.
  • Six apps, one platform: Same underlying AI, packaged for different jobs — so customers can start with one and add more later.
  • Sells to businesses and consumers: Enterprise annual contracts (B2B) plus affordable monthly subscriptions for small users (B2C).
